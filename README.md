# 📊 Madhav E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI Sales Dashboard** designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product categories, and payment methods. This dashboard provides business stakeholders with a comprehensive overview of key performance metrics and supports data-driven decision-making through interactive visualizations and filters.

# 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw e-commerce sales data into meaningful business insights using Power BI. The dashboard enables users to monitor overall business performance, identify high-performing products and regions, analyze customer purchasing behavior, and track monthly profit trends.

# 📈 Business Insights

- Maharashtra generated the highest sales among all states.
- Clothing was the best-selling product category by quantity.
- Printers produced the highest profit among all sub-categories.
- Profit was highest during the beginning and end of the year, with losses occurring in several mid-year months.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) was the most preferred payment method.
